# Catch a sound, then choose what it becomes

In **Sound → Capture / hum**, record a microphone take or open a WAV up to 30 seconds long. Hum one voice at a time, hold a vowel, or play a single instrument. Review happens before anything is added to your music.

- **Cycle → wave A / B** takes a stable repeating cycle into the Wave Shaper as 32 editable dots. The other wave, pitches, effects and smoothness keep their settings. Shape its peaks, troughs and edges, then **Keep patch tone** to save the full two-voice recipe. This repeating outline is a new instrument, not a recording of your voice.
- **Add editable notes** turns a single-voice hum into pitches, rests and timing. Choose Melody, Chords or Bass, an insertion measure, nearest-note or scale tuning, and a timing grid. Keep my timing preserves unquantized timing. Notes use the chosen part's current sound and the Melody key. Existing notes remain; duplicate positions are skipped. A phrase that exceeds 16 measures or the part's note limit is rejected in full.
- **Recording → song cursor** keeps the original performance as a movable audio clip. This uses the song's current cursor, not the hum insertion measure. The existing eight-recording / one-minute combined limit applies.
- **Save original WAV** keeps a separate local copy. The little preview plays the first three seconds, once.

The reviewed take stays in Capture while you move between tools. It is temporary until you add it to the song or save its WAV. Editable hum notes and patch outlines do not contain the original recording. A saved Studio project includes the recordings you explicitly put in the song.

Pitch tracking works best for one steady voice or instrument, roughly C2–B5. Chords, unpitched noise and background music may not produce clear notes or a repeating cycle; the original recording remains usable. This is pitch extraction, not lyric transcription.

Microphone capture requires a supported browser and a secure page (HTTPS or localhost). On an HTTP Home Assistant connection, use WAV import or the existing trusted HTTPS address. Microphone permission is requested only after Record. Stop, Cancel, leaving Capture, hiding the page, or closing it releases the microphone. Nothing is uploaded.

The making path stays visible through **Capture → Patch → Beat → Melody / Vocals → Arrange → Export**. Its counts describe the sounds and music actually present. You can enter at any step; no checklist blocks your work. WOOFpad, original sounds, editable patterns and independent arrangement copies stay reusable.
