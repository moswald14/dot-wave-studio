# WOOFpad and patch tones

Open **Sound → WOOFpad**. It is part of the main studio again. Typing **WOOF** outside a text field also opens it.

**Start wandering** discovers new pairs inside your chosen pitch range. Choose the collision and how often it changes. Turn off **Wander the outlines too** to keep your drawings while exploring pitches. **Catch this tone** pauses at the current patch; **New collision** finds one without starting continuous playback. One uninterrupted wander is one Undo edit. Sessions stop after a minute, and Stop all or Escape cancels pending audio too.

Name a discovery and press **Keep patch tone**. The sound box retains the two outlines, their smoothness and pins, both pitches, collision, warmth, polarity and effects. Selecting a kept patch tone restores both editable voices. **Keep & use in melody** assigns it to the selected melody part. Its notes transpose the two voices together; saved patterns, arrangement placements and WAV/stem exports retain their sound.

**Patch tones** now leads the Sound step. Set Base A and Color B directly. **Link tuning** moves both pitches by the same factor. Interval buttons set unison, a slow beating pair, a fifth, an octave or a bell-like pair. The Wave Shaper remains below for precise outline editing. Each source and kept sound has its three-second preview.

**Play the patch** uses held keys, mouse/touch or A W S E D F T G Y H U J K when the keyboard has focus. Choose one to three octaves and shift the range. **Concert** puts A4 at 440 Hz. **C4 = patch root** keeps the patch's original pitches at C4. Notes outside the audio device's range are disabled rather than changing the ratio. Up to eight keys sound together; an individual held key stops after fifteen seconds.

**Capture for keys** freezes the keyboard's recipe while you continue exploring. **Follow patch** reconnects it to the editor. **Save captured keys** keeps a portable recipe. **Save patch file** exports the current editor. **Open patch file** accepts new patch tones, original Classic patch files and original WOOF switchboards with their tuning and octave shift.

The **Phone tones** keypad seeds two sine waves with the original twelve touch-tone pairs. Numpad 0–9 and * work in WOOFpad outside text fields; numpad / plays # and decimal stops. The sequence field plays up to 32 digits. Dial tone uses 350 + 440 Hz. These are musical tones; no calling connection is used.

The latest 100 **Discoveries** stay in this browser. Like a tone, browse older/newer entries, recall its exact recipe, or press its **3s** player without loading it. **Export history** makes a backup. Discovery history stays outside project files; only tones you deliberately keep enter the sound box. Existing Classic WOOF history on this website is recognized.

Save a Studio 2.6 project to keep editable patch tones. Classic export refuses recipes it cannot preserve; WAV is the audio handoff. Browser drafts are separate on each website. Microphone input, real hardware output and physical touch devices are separate checks.
